Percentage

Percentage is an important topic in SSC CGL Quantitative Aptitude. It is used in questions involving profit and loss, discounts, interest, marks, population and many other topics.

1. Basic Concept

Percentage means "per hundred".

Percentage = (Part / Whole) × 100

Example: If a student scores 45 marks out of 60:

Percentage = (45 / 60) × 100 = 75%

2. Important Percentage Conversions

  • 1% = 1/100
  • 10% = 1/10
  • 20% = 1/5
  • 25% = 1/4
  • 50% = 1/2
  • 75% = 3/4
  • 100% = 1

3. Percentage Increase

Percentage Increase = (Increase / Original Value) × 100

Example: A price increases from ₹200 to ₹250.

Increase = ₹50

Percentage Increase = (50 / 200) × 100 = 25%

4. Percentage Decrease

Percentage Decrease = (Decrease / Original Value) × 100

Example: A price decreases from ₹500 to ₹400.

Decrease = ₹100

Percentage Decrease = (100 / 500) × 100 = 20%

5. Successive Percentage Change

For two successive changes of a% and b%:

Net Change = a + b + (ab / 100)

Use the signs carefully:

  • Increase → positive
  • Decrease → negative

6. Important Shortcuts

  • 50% of a number = half of the number.
  • 25% of a number = one-fourth of the number.
  • 10% of a number = divide by 10.
  • 5% of a number = 10% ÷ 2.
  • 20% of a number = one-fifth of the number.

7. SSC CGL Practice Questions

Question 1: What is 25% of 240?

  • 40
  • 50
  • 60
  • 80

Question 2: A number increases from 200 to 250. What is the percentage increase?

  • 20%
  • 25%
  • 30%
  • 35%

Question 3: 75% of a number is 150. What is the number?

  • 180
  • 200
  • 220
  • 250

Answer Key

  • Question 1 → 60
  • Question 2 → 25%
  • Question 3 → 200
