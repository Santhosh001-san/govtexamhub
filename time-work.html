Time and Work

Time and Work is an important arithmetic topic in SSC CGL. Questions are mainly based on work efficiency, number of workers, time taken and combined work.

1. Basic Concept

If a person completes a work in a certain number of days, we can calculate the work done in one day.

One Day's Work = 1 / Total Days

Example: A can complete a work in 10 days.

A's one day's work = 1/10

2. Work Formula

Work = Efficiency × Time

Therefore:

Efficiency = Work / Time

Time = Work / Efficiency

3. Working Together

If A completes a work in 10 days and B completes it in 15 days:

A's one-day work = 1/10

B's one-day work = 1/15

Together:

1/10 + 1/15 = 1/6

Therefore, they complete the work together in 6 days.

4. Efficiency and Time

For the same amount of work, efficiency and time are inversely proportional.

Efficiency × Time = Constant

Example: If A is twice as efficient as B, A will take half the time taken by B.

5. Work and Wages

When workers are paid according to the amount of work completed, wages are proportional to the work done.

Wages Ratio = Work Ratio

6. Important Shortcut

If A completes a work in x days and B completes it in y days, their combined time is:

Combined Time = (x × y) / (x + y)

Example: A = 10 days, B = 15 days.

Combined Time = (10 × 15) / (10 + 15)

= 150 / 25 = 6 days

7. SSC CGL Practice Questions

Question 1: A can complete a work in 10 days. What part of the work does A complete in one day?

  • 1/5
  • 1/10
  • 1/15
  • 1/20

Question 2: A can complete a work in 10 days and B can complete it in 15 days. How many days will they take together?

  • 5 days
  • 6 days
  • 7 days
  • 8 days

Question 3: A is twice as efficient as B. If B takes 20 days to complete a work, how many days will A take?

  • 5 days
  • 10 days
  • 15 days
  • 20 days

Answer Key

  • Question 1 → 1/10
  • Question 2 → 6 days
  • Question 3 → 10 days
