Mensuration

Mensuration is an important topic in SSC CGL Quantitative Aptitude. It deals with the measurement of geometrical figures such as area, perimeter, surface area and volume.

1. Important 2D Formulas

Square

Perimeter = 4 × Side

Area = Side²

Rectangle

Perimeter = 2 × (Length + Breadth)

Area = Length × Breadth

Triangle

Area = 1/2 × Base × Height

Circle

Circumference = 2πr

Area = πr²

2. Important 3D Formulas

Cube

Volume = Side³

Total Surface Area = 6 × Side²

Cuboid

Volume = Length × Breadth × Height

Total Surface Area = 2(lb + bh + hl)

Cylinder

Volume = πr²h

Curved Surface Area = 2πrh

Total Surface Area = 2πr(r + h)

3. Cone

Volume = 1/3 πr²h

Curved Surface Area = πrl

Here, l represents the slant height.

4. Sphere

Volume = 4/3 πr³

Surface Area = 4πr²

5. Important Value of π

In many SSC CGL questions, you can use:

π = 22/7

or

π = 3.14

Use the value given in the question whenever specified.

6. Example

Find the area of a rectangle whose length is 12 cm and breadth is 5 cm.

Area = Length × Breadth

= 12 × 5

Area = 60 cm²

7. SSC CGL Practice Questions

Question 1: Find the area of a square whose side is 8 cm.

  • 32 cm²
  • 48 cm²
  • 64 cm²
  • 72 cm²

Question 2: Find the area of a triangle with base 10 cm and height 8 cm.

  • 30 cm²
  • 40 cm²
  • 50 cm²
  • 80 cm²

Question 3: Find the volume of a cube whose side is 5 cm.

  • 25 cm³
  • 75 cm³
  • 100 cm³
  • 125 cm³

Answer Key

  • Question 1 → 64 cm²
  • Question 2 → 40 cm²
  • Question 3 → 125 cm³
