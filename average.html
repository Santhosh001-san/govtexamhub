Average

Average is an important topic in SSC CGL Quantitative Aptitude. It is commonly used in questions involving marks, ages, salaries, numbers and other quantities.

1. Basic Formula

Average = Sum of all values ÷ Number of values

Example:

Find the average of 10, 20 and 30.

Average = (10 + 20 + 30) ÷ 3

Average = 20

2. Finding the Total

If average and number of values are known:

Total = Average × Number of values

Example: The average of 5 numbers is 18.

Total = 18 × 5 = 90

3. Finding a Missing Number

First calculate the total using:

Total = Average × Number of values

Then subtract the known values from the total.

Example: The average of 5 numbers is 20. Four numbers are 12, 18, 22 and 25.

Total = 20 × 5 = 100

Known total = 12 + 18 + 22 + 25 = 77

Missing number = 100 - 77 = 23

4. Average of Consecutive Numbers

For consecutive numbers, the average is the middle number when the number of terms is odd.

Example: 11, 12, 13, 14, 15

Average = 13

For an even number of consecutive terms, the average is the average of the two middle numbers.

5. Change in Average

When one value is added or removed, compare the change in the total with the change in the number of values.

This method is useful for solving SSC CGL average questions quickly.

6. Important Shortcut

If every value in a group is increased by the same number, the average also increases by that number.

Example: If every number increases by 5, the average also increases by 5.

7. SSC CGL Practice Questions
