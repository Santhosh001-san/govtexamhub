Time, Speed and Distance

Time, Speed and Distance is an important topic in SSC CGL. Questions are commonly based on speed, distance, time, unit conversion, average speed and relative speed.

1. Basic Formula

Speed = Distance / Time

Distance = Speed × Time

Time = Distance / Speed

2. Example

A car travels 120 km in 3 hours. Find its speed.

Speed = 120 / 3

Speed = 40 km/h

3. Unit Conversion

The most important conversions for SSC CGL are:

  • 1 km = 1000 metres
  • 1 hour = 3600 seconds
  • 1 minute = 60 seconds

To convert km/h into m/s:

km/h × 5/18 = m/s

To convert m/s into km/h:

m/s × 18/5 = km/h

Example: 72 km/h = 72 × 5/18 = 20 m/s

4. Average Speed

Average speed is:

Average Speed = Total Distance / Total Time

When equal distances are travelled at speeds x and y:

Average Speed = 2xy / (x + y)

5. Relative Speed

When two objects move in the same direction:

Relative Speed = Difference of speeds

When two objects move in opposite directions:

Relative Speed = Sum of speeds

Example: Two vehicles move in opposite directions at 40 km/h and 50 km/h.

Relative speed = 40 + 50 = 90 km/h

6. Important Shortcut

If speed increases, the time required for the same distance decreases.

For the same distance:

Speed × Time = Constant

Therefore, speed and time are inversely proportional.

7. SSC CGL Practice Questions

Question 1: A train travels 180 km in 3 hours. Find its speed.

  • 50 km/h
  • 60 km/h
  • 70 km/h
  • 80 km/h

Question 2: Convert 54 km/h into m/s.

  • 10 m/s
  • 12 m/s
  • 15 m/s
  • 18 m/s

Question 3: A person travels equal distances at 40 km/h and 60 km/h. Find the average speed.

  • 45 km/h
  • 48 km/h
  • 50 km/h
  • 52 km/h

Answer Key

  • Question 1 → 60 km/h
  • Question 2 → 15 m/s
  • Question 3 → 48 km/h
