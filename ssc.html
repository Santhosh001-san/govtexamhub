SSC CGL Exam Overview

SSC CGL is a major competitive examination conducted by the Staff Selection Commission for recruitment to various Group B and Group C posts in the Government of India.

  • Conducting body: Staff Selection Commission
  • Exam: Combined Graduate Level Examination
  • Level: Graduate
  • Selection: Multiple stages depending on the post
