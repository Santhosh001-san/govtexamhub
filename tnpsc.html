TNPSC Exam Overview

TNPSC conducts various exams to recruit personnel for state administrative and ministerial service posts in Tamil Nadu. The syllabus places a heavy emphasis on Aptitude, General Studies, and the History & Culture of Tamil Nadu.

  • Conducting body: Tamil Nadu Public Service Commission
  • Exam Level: Graduate (Group 1, 2), SSLC/10th (Group 4)
  • Selection Process: Prelims -> Mains -> Oral Test (Or Single Written Exam for Group 4)
  • Key Parameter: High performance in Tamil Culture and Mental Ability

TNPSC Preparation Strategy

  1. Rely heavily on Tamil Nadu School Education (Samacheer Kalvi) textbooks from Class 6 to 12.
  2. Give utmost importance to Aptitude & Mental Ability, as it carries a fixed 25 questions in Group exams.
  3. Understand Unit 8 (History, Culture & Heritage of TN) and Unit 9 (Development Admin in TN) deeply.
  4. Study Thirukkural in detail, including its significance in modern society, administration, and ethics.
  5. Read regional news daily to stay updated with Tamil Nadu state government welfare schemes and budget allocations.
  6. Solve previous years' TNPSC Group question papers to gauge question patterns.
