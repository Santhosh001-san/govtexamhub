Simple Interest

Simple Interest is an important topic in SSC CGL Quantitative Aptitude. Questions are mainly based on Principal, Rate, Time, Interest and Amount.

1. Important Terms

  • Principal (P): The original amount invested or borrowed.
  • Rate (R): Interest rate per year.
  • Time (T): Period for which money is invested or borrowed.
  • Simple Interest (SI): Interest calculated only on the original principal.
  • Amount (A): Principal + Interest.

2. Simple Interest Formula

SI = (P × R × T) / 100

Example:

P = ₹5000, R = 10%, T = 2 years

SI = (5000 × 10 × 2) / 100

SI = ₹1000

3. Amount Formula

Amount = Principal + Simple Interest

Using the above example:

Amount = ₹5000 + ₹1000

Amount = ₹6000

4. Finding Principal

From:

SI = (P × R × T) / 100

We get:

P = (SI × 100) / (R × T)

5. Finding Rate

R = (SI × 100) / (P × T)

6. Finding Time

T = (SI × 100) / (P × R)

7. Important Shortcut

If the rate is given for months, convert the time into years.

For example:

6 months = 6/12 = 1/2 year

9 months = 9/12 = 3/4 year

3 months = 3/12 = 1/4 year

8. SSC CGL Practice Questions

Question 1: Find the simple interest on ₹5000 at 10% per annum for 2 years.

  • ₹500
  • ₹750
  • ₹1000
  • ₹1200

Question 2: Find the amount when the principal is ₹4000 and simple interest is ₹800.

  • ₹4200
  • ₹4500
  • ₹4800
  • ₹5000

Question 3: Find the simple interest on ₹2000 at 5% per annum for 3 years.

  • ₹200
  • ₹250
  • ₹300
  • ₹350

Answer Key

  • Question 1 → ₹1000
  • Question 2 → ₹4800
  • Question 3 → ₹300
