Statistics

Statistics is an important mathematical topic involving the collection, organization and interpretation of data. SSC CGL questions commonly involve mean, median and mode.

1. Mean

Mean is commonly called the average.

Mean = Sum of all observations / Number of observations

Example: Find the mean of 10, 20 and 30.

Mean = (10 + 20 + 30) / 3

Mean = 20

2. Median

Median is the middle value when the observations are arranged in ascending or descending order.

Example:

10, 20, 30, 40, 50

Median = 30

If the number of observations is even:

Median = Average of the two middle values

3. Mode

Mode is the value that occurs most frequently in a data set.

Example:

2, 4, 4, 5, 6, 4, 8

Since 4 occurs most frequently:

Mode = 4

4. Important Relation

For a moderately skewed distribution:

Mode = 3 × Median − 2 × Mean

This relation can be useful in SSC-level questions.

5. Change in Mean

If the same number is added to every observation, the mean also increases by that number.

Example:

Original values: 10, 20, 30

Mean = 20

Add 5 to every value: 15, 25, 35

New mean = 25

6. Weighted Average

When different values have different weights:

Weighted Average = Σ(Value × Weight) / ΣWeight

This is useful when quantities have different importance or frequencies.

7. SSC CGL Practice Questions

Question 1: Find the mean of 10, 20, 30, 40 and 50.

  • 20
  • 25
  • 30
  • 35

Question 2: Find the median of: 12, 18, 20, 25, 30

  • 18
  • 20
  • 25
  • 30

Question 3: Find the mode of: 5, 7, 8, 7, 9, 7, 10

  • 5
  • 7
  • 8
  • 10

Answer Key

  • Question 1 → 30
  • Question 2 → 20
  • Question 3 → 7
