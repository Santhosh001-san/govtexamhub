Profit and Loss

Profit and Loss is an important arithmetic topic for SSC CGL. Questions are commonly asked using Cost Price, Selling Price, profit percentage, loss percentage and discount.

1. Important Terms

  • Cost Price (CP): Price at which an article is purchased.
  • Selling Price (SP): Price at which an article is sold.
  • Profit: When SP is greater than CP.
  • Loss: When SP is less than CP.
  • Marked Price (MP): Price written on the article before discount.

2. Profit Formula

Profit = SP − CP

Profit% = (Profit / CP) × 100

Example: CP = ₹500 and SP = ₹600.

Profit = 600 − 500 = ₹100

Profit% = (100 / 500) × 100 = 20%

3. Loss Formula

Loss = CP − SP

Loss% = (Loss / CP) × 100

Example: CP = ₹800 and SP = ₹720.

Loss = 800 − 720 = ₹80

Loss% = (80 / 800) × 100 = 10%

4. Finding Selling Price

If there is a profit of x%:

SP = CP × (100 + x) / 100

If there is a loss of x%:

SP = CP × (100 − x) / 100

5. Finding Cost Price

If an article is sold at x% profit:

CP = SP × 100 / (100 + x)

If an article is sold at x% loss:

CP = SP × 100 / (100 − x)

6. Discount

Discount = Marked Price − Selling Price

Discount% = (Discount / Marked Price) × 100

Example: MP = ₹1000 and discount = 20%.

Discount = ₹200

SP = ₹1000 − ₹200 = ₹800

7. Important Shortcut

If an article is sold at a profit of 25%, then:

SP : CP = 125 : 100 = 5 : 4

If an article is sold at a loss of 20%, then:

SP : CP = 80 : 100 = 4 : 5

8. SSC CGL Practice Questions

Question 1: An article is bought for ₹500 and sold for ₹600. Find the profit percentage.

  • 10%
  • 15%
  • 20%
  • 25%

Question 2: An article costing ₹800 is sold at a loss of 10%. Find the selling price.

  • ₹700
  • ₹720
  • ₹740
  • ₹760

Question 3: An article marked at ₹1000 is sold after a discount of 20%. Find the selling price.

  • ₹750
  • ₹800
  • ₹850
  • ₹900

Answer Key

  • Question 1 → 20%
  • Question 2 → ₹720
  • Question 3 → ₹800
