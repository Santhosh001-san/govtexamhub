Geometry

Geometry is an important topic in SSC CGL Quantitative Aptitude. Questions are commonly based on lines, angles, triangles, quadrilaterals, circles and important geometric properties.

1. Lines and Angles

  • Complementary Angles: Two angles whose sum is 90°.
  • Supplementary Angles: Two angles whose sum is 180°.
  • Linear Pair: Two adjacent angles whose sum is 180°.
  • Vertically Opposite Angles: They are always equal.

Example: If one angle of a linear pair is 70°, the other angle is:

180° − 70° = 110°

2. Triangle

A triangle has three sides and three angles.

Sum of angles of a triangle = 180°

Example: If two angles are 50° and 60°, the third angle is:

180° − (50° + 60°) = 70°

3. Types of Triangles

  • Equilateral Triangle: All three sides are equal and each angle is 60°.
  • Isosceles Triangle: Two sides are equal.
  • Scalene Triangle: All three sides are different.
  • Right-Angled Triangle: One angle is 90°.

4. Pythagoras Theorem

In a right-angled triangle:

Hypotenuse² = Base² + Height²

Example: If the base is 3 cm and height is 4 cm:

Hypotenuse² = 3² + 4²

= 9 + 16 = 25

Hypotenuse = 5 cm

5. Quadrilaterals

A quadrilateral has four sides.

Sum of interior angles = 360°

  • Square: Four equal sides and four right angles.
  • Rectangle: Opposite sides are equal and all angles are 90°.
  • Parallelogram: Opposite sides are parallel and equal.
  • Rhombus: All four sides are equal.
  • Trapezium: One pair of opposite sides is parallel.

6. Circle

Important circle formulas:

Diameter = 2 × Radius

Circumference = 2πr

Area = πr²

Where r is the radius.

7. Important Geometry Properties

  • Angles in a straight line add up to 180°.
  • Angles around a point add up to 360°.
  • Vertically opposite angles are equal.
  • The sum of angles of a triangle is 180°.
  • The exterior angle of a triangle equals the sum of the two opposite interior angles.
  • The sum of interior angles of a quadrilateral is 360°.

8. SSC CGL Practice Questions

Question 1: Two angles of a triangle are 50° and 60°. Find the third angle.

  • 60°
  • 70°
  • 80°
  • 90°

Question 2: A right-angled triangle has sides 6 cm and 8 cm forming the right angle. Find the hypotenuse.

  • 9 cm
  • 10 cm
  • 12 cm
  • 14 cm

Question 3: Find the circumference of a circle with radius 7 cm. Use π = 22/7.

  • 22 cm
  • 44 cm
  • 49 cm
  • 154 cm

Answer Key

  • Question 1 → 70°
  • Question 2 → 10 cm
  • Question 3 → 44 cm
