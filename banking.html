Banking Exam Overview

Banking exams are conducted online across various stages (Prelims and Mains) to recruit officers, clerks, and specialists. Speed, accuracy, and strong fundamentals are key to cracking banking exams.

  • Conducting body: IBPS, SBI, RBI
  • Exam Level: Graduate (PO), Intermediate/Graduate (Clerk)
  • Selection Process: Prelims -> Mains -> Interview (for PO)
  • Key Parameter: Sectional timing and high accuracy

Banking Preparation Strategy

  1. Master the basics of percentage, ratio, and average to speed up Quantitative Aptitude.
  2. Learn short tricks for simplification, square roots, and cube roots.
  3. Practice circular, linear, and square seating arrangements daily to build speed.
  4. Read daily editorials from standard newspapers to improve English comprehension.
  5. Keep a note of daily banking news, RBI policy changes, and financial schemes.
  6. Attempt online sectional mock tests daily to analyze speed and accuracy.
