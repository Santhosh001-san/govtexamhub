UPSC Exam Overview

The Civil Services Examination (CSE) is conducted by the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) to recruit candidates for civil services like IAS, IPS, IFS, and IRS. The Prelims exam consists of two objective papers: General Studies I and CSAT.

  • Conducting body: Union Public Service Commission
  • Exam Level: Graduate
  • Selection Process: Prelims -> Mains -> Interview
  • Key Parameter: Conceptual understanding and analytical capability

Important Subjects

  • CSAT (Paper II)Aptitude, Reasoning, Comprehension

UPSC Preparation Strategy

  1. Read standard NCERT textbooks (Class 6-12) to build a solid foundation of General Studies.
  2. Correlate current affairs with dynamic sections of Polity, Economy, and Environment.
  3. Analyze previous years' UPSC Prelims question papers to identify the pattern and nature of statements.
  4. Practice writing answers for Mains alongside Prelims prep to consolidate concepts.
  5. Do not ignore CSAT; solve at least 2 years' worth of CSAT papers to confirm CSAT qualifying status (33%).
  6. Attempt full-length prelims mock tests to manage time and negative marking effectively.
