Ratio and Proportion

Ratio and Proportion is an important topic in SSC CGL Quantitative Aptitude. It is commonly used in questions involving quantities, ages, mixtures, partnership and comparisons.

1. What is a Ratio?

A ratio compares two quantities of the same kind.

It is written in the form:

a : b

Example: If there are 20 boys and 30 girls:

Boys : Girls = 20 : 30 = 2 : 3

2. Important Rules of Ratio

  • Both quantities must be in the same unit.
  • Ratio has no unit.
  • Both terms can be multiplied or divided by the same non-zero number.
  • 2 : 3 = 4 : 6 = 6 : 9.

3. Simplifying a Ratio

Divide both terms by their Highest Common Factor (HCF).

Example:

24 : 36

HCF = 12

24 : 36 = 2 : 3

4. What is Proportion?

When two ratios are equal, they form a proportion.

Example:

2 : 3 = 4 : 6

Therefore, 2, 3, 4 and 6 are in proportion.

5. Fourth Proportional

If:

a : b = c : x

Then:

x = (b × c) / a

Example:

2 : 5 = 8 : x

x = (5 × 8) / 2 = 20

6. Direct Proportion

In direct proportion, when one quantity increases, the other quantity also increases in the same relationship.

Example: More items generally require more money when the price per item remains constant.

7. Inverse Proportion

In inverse proportion, when one quantity increases, the other quantity decreases.

Example: For a fixed amount of work, more workers generally require less time to complete the work.

8. Important Shortcut

If:

a : b = m : n

You can write:

a = mk and b = nk

where k is a common multiplier.

9. SSC CGL Practice Questions

Question 1: Simplify the ratio 24 : 36.

  • 1 : 2
  • 2 : 3
  • 3 : 4
  • 4 : 5

Question 2: If 2 : 5 = 8 : x, find x.

  • 10
  • 15
  • 20
  • 25

Question 3: The ratio of boys to girls is 3 : 2. If there are 30 boys, how many girls are there?

  • 15
  • 20
  • 25
  • 30

Answer Key

  • Question 1 → 2 : 3
  • Question 2 → 20
  • Question 3 → 20
