Compound Interest

Compound Interest is an important topic in SSC CGL. Unlike Simple Interest, compound interest is calculated on the principal as well as the accumulated interest.

1. Important Terms

  • Principal (P): Original amount.
  • Rate (R): Rate of interest per period.
  • Time (T): Number of periods.
  • Amount (A): Principal + Compound Interest.
  • Compound Interest (CI): Amount − Principal.

2. Compound Interest Formula

When interest is compounded annually:

A = P(1 + R/100)T

Then:

CI = A − P

Example:

P = ₹10,000, R = 10%, T = 2 years

A = 10000 × (1 + 10/100)2

A = 10000 × 1.21 = ₹12,100

CI = 12,100 − 10,000

CI = ₹2,100

3. Compounding Half-Yearly

When interest is compounded half-yearly:

  • Rate becomes R/2
  • Time becomes 2T

Formula:

A = P(1 + R/200)2T

4. Compounding Quarterly

When interest is compounded quarterly:

  • Rate becomes R/4
  • Time becomes 4T

Formula:

A = P(1 + R/400)4T

5. Difference Between SI and CI

For 2 years, when the rate is R% per annum:

CI − SI = P × (R/100)2

Example: P = ₹10,000 and R = 10%.

Difference = 10000 × (10/100)2

Difference = ₹100

6. Important Shortcut

For 2 years:

CI = P × [2R/100 + R²/10000]

This shortcut can save time in SSC CGL questions.

7. SSC CGL Practice Questions

Question 1: Find the compound interest on ₹10,000 at 10% per annum for 2 years, compounded annually.

  • ₹1,000
  • ₹2,000
  • ₹2,100
  • ₹2,200

Question 2: Find the amount on ₹5,000 at 10% per annum for 2 years, compounded annually.

  • ₹5,500
  • ₹6,000
  • ₹6,050
  • ₹6,100

Question 3: What is the difference between CI and SI on ₹10,000 at 10% per annum for 2 years?

  • ₹50
  • ₹100
  • ₹150
  • ₹200

Answer Key

  • Question 1 → ₹2,100
  • Question 2 → ₹6,050
  • Question 3 → ₹100
