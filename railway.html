Railway Exam Overview

Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) conducts exams nationwide to recruit candidates for various technical and non-technical posts. Speed, accuracy, and general science knowledge are crucial parameters.

  • Conducting body: Railway Recruitment Board (RRB)
  • Exam Level: 10th, 12th, Graduate (depending on post)
  • Selection Process: CBT 1 -> CBT 2 -> Skill Test / Physical Test
  • Key Parameter: High accuracy in General Science and Mathematics

Railway Preparation Strategy

  1. Prepare Class 9 and 10 CBSE NCERT Science books thoroughly, as General Science forms a core pillar.
  2. Practice simplification and basic mathematics calculation rules (BODMAS) to solve math questions rapidly.
  3. Solve previous year RRB question papers to understand the trend of general awareness questions.
  4. Learn the history and facts of Indian Railways (such as the first train, division zones, and current rail budgets).
  5. Attempt online computer-based tests (CBT) regularly to get accustomed to standard exam interface timings.
